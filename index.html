Personal Vault

A private, end-to-end encrypted vault for passwords, two-factor codes, secure notes and documents.

Zero-knowledgeEverything is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. Your master password never leaves it.
Your own Google DriveOptional encrypted backup in a hidden app folder of your Drive. Google can't read it.
Security CenterFinds leaked, weak and reused passwords and accounts without 2FA.
Biometrics & PINUnlock with your fingerprint, face or PIN. Auto-locks when you leave.

Personal Vault is a personal, non-commercial app. It has no servers, accounts, ads or analytics.
