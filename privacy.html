Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Personal Vault ("the app") is a personal password and document vault. It is built so that nobody except you — including the app's developer — can read what you store in it.

What the app stores

  • Everything you add (accounts, passwords, two-factor secrets, notes, documents and files) is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is saved. The encryption key is protected by your master password (Argon2id) and, if you enable them, by your device's secure hardware for fingerprint/face and PIN unlock.
  • Your master password and decrypted data never leave your device.
  • The app has no servers, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Google account and Google Drive (optional)

  • If you choose "Link Google Drive", the app signs in with your Google account and requests only the drive.appdata permission. This lets it create and read files in a hidden folder that belongs to the app; it cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Only already-encrypted data is uploaded to that folder, to back up and sync your vault between your devices. Google cannot read it.
  • The app uses your Google account email only to show which account is linked, on your device. It does not share Google user data with anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can unlink at any time in the app, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Optional online checks

  • Leaked-password check — uses the Have I Been Pwned "Pwned Passwords" range API with k-anonymity: only the first 5 characters of a SHA-1 hash of a password are sent, never the password or its full hash.
  • Website logos — if enabled, the app downloads site icons from DuckDuckGo's icon service, which receives the website domain name (for example github.com) but no login details. This can be turned off in Settings.

Deleting your data

"Erase vault from this device" in Settings deletes all local data. Unlinking Google Drive stops syncing; the encrypted backup can be deleted in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → Personal Vault → "Delete hidden app data".
